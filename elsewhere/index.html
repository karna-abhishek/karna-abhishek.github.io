Creative Projects

Mycopoetica

2022

A poetry collection exploring fungi's role in climate change through the lens of absurdist philosophy and thematic verse. Ten poems structured to provoke critical reflection on ecological collapse, decomposition, and regeneration, with planned multimedia extensions. Written for Performing Science: Experimentation, Collaboration, and Artistry, my freshman fall course at Duke.

Sacred Architectures

2023–24

Field research documenting over 100 temples across South India and more than 15 in Cambodia, tracing the architectural and religious exchange between Dravidian and Khmer traditions. The project produced a multimedia poster, South Asia's Sacred Temples: A Dive into the Ethno-Geographical and Transcultural Religious Exchange, and a documentary-style trailer on the Dravidian–Khmer architectural dialogue.

Founding President. Brought together 40+ Nepali-origin students and staff at Duke, organized 10+ cultural and community events, networked with the Nepali diaspora across North Carolina, and engaged the broader Duke community with Nepali culture.

Duke in Geneva

2024

Studied International Business and the Political Philosophy of Globalization as part of Duke's Study Away Program. Visited the UN, Palais des Nations, UNHCR, ILO, WHO, WTO, and CERN. Traveled across Switzerland, France, Italy, and several other European countries for field experiences in Philosophy, Politics, and Economics.
