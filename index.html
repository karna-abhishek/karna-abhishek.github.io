Abhishek Karna

PhD student in physics, Princeton University

I work on machine learning for quantum materials with B. Andrei Bernevig.

Before Princeton I earned a BS in Physics and a BS in Mathematics at Duke University, with a certificate in Philosophy, Politics and Economics. At Duke I developed a quantum Monte Carlo method for lattice Hamiltonians with Shailesh Chandrasekharan, and synthesized and grew crystals of quantum magnets in Sara Haravifard's lab. I also worked on air pollution in Nepal with Brian McAdoo at PlanetLab.
